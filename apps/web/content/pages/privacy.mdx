## Information we collect

When you create or use an account, we may collect your email address, username, profile details,
country, authentication records, and information needed to keep your account secure.

We also receive information about purchases and the products you access so that we can complete
orders, show your history, provide access, and help resolve questions.

If you add funds or use an external payment method, the selected payment provider handles the
payment information required for that transaction. We receive the transaction details needed to
confirm its status and support your account.

If you choose to use referrals, we record information needed to create referral links, attribute
qualifying purchases, show referral activity, and process eligible withdrawals.

Technical information such as device details, browser information, approximate location, logs,
and security events may be collected to operate and protect the service.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- provide and secure accounts;
- complete purchases and provide the access described by listings;
- process wallet funding and communicate transaction status;
- support optional referrals and withdrawals;
- respond to support requests;
- detect fraud, abuse, and security threats; and
- meet operational and legal obligations that apply to the service.

## Service providers

We use trusted providers for services such as authentication, payment processing, email delivery,
hosting, storage, monitoring, and security. These providers receive only the information needed to
perform their service and handle it under their own applicable terms and privacy notices.

## Retention and choices

We keep information for as long as it is needed to provide the service, maintain transaction and
account records, resolve disputes, protect the platform, and meet applicable obligations. You can
contact us to ask questions about your information or request changes where the law and our ability
to provide the service allow it.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No online service can
guarantee absolute security, so protect your password and contact us promptly if you suspect
unauthorized access.

We may update this policy as the service changes. The version published on this page includes its
effective update date. For privacy questions or requests, contact <SupportEmail />.
