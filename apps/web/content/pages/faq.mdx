## What can I find here?

The catalogue contains digital products and resources, such as software access, learning material,
templates, and other useful tools. Each listing explains what it includes.

## Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse the public catalogue and read listing details without an account. An account is
needed to complete a purchase and access products you have bought.

## How do I buy a product?

Open a listing, review its details, and select **Buy now**. If you are not signed in, we will ask
you to sign in or create an account before continuing.

## How do I pay or add funds?

Your account wallet is used for purchases. When more funds are needed, choose an available payment
method and follow the provider's instructions. Your purchase continues after the payment is
confirmed.

## Where do I find products I already bought?

Sign in and open your dashboard. Your purchases and their access details are available there.

## Can I sell my own products?

No. The catalogue is managed by <SiteName />. Members use it to discover, buy, access, and share
eligible products.

## How do referrals work?

Some eligible listings can be shared with an attributed referral link. If someone completes a
qualifying purchase through your link, the applicable referral policy may provide a commission.
Clicks alone do not create earnings.

## Do I have to promote products?

No. Promotion is completely optional. You can use the service only to discover and buy products.

## How do I withdraw referral earnings?

Eligible earnings become available according to the current referral and withdrawal policies. From
your dashboard, you can review available earnings and request a withdrawal when the requirements
shown there are met.

## How do I get help?

For account, purchase, access, or payment questions, contact <SupportEmail />. Never send a
password, API key, or payment secret in a support message.
