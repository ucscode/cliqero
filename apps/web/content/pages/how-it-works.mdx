title: How it works
description: Find a digital product, buy access, and use it when you need it.
updated: 2026-09-06
---

## 1. Find a product

Browse the catalogue and open a listing to review its description, price, media, and access
details. Search and filters help you narrow the options.

## 2. Buy it

Choose **Buy now** when you are ready. You can add money securely using an available payment method
if your account needs more funds, then continue with the purchase.

## 3. Access it

After a successful purchase, the product appears in your account with the access information
described on its listing. Follow those instructions to get started.

## 4. Share eligible products

If a product supports referrals, you can share its attributed link with people who may find it
useful. A qualifying purchase can earn a commission under the current referral policy. You never
need to promote anything to use the catalogue.
