---
title: How Cliqero works
description: Discover products, buy what you need, access your purchases, and optionally earn by sharing eligible products.
---

Cliqero keeps the journey simple: find something useful, buy it, and get access from your account.

## 1. Explore the catalogue

Browse the catalogue to discover digital products and resources available on Cliqero. Open any item to see its description, price, media, and other details before deciding whether it is right for you.

## 2. Buy what you want

When you are ready, choose **Buy**. If your available purchase value is enough, the purchase can be completed immediately. If more funds are needed, Cliqero will guide you through adding them before the purchase continues.

You do not need to understand the payment mechanics behind the platform. The checkout experience is designed around the product you are buying.

## 3. Access your purchase

Completed purchases stay connected to your account. Visit your purchases to return to products you have access to without keeping track of delivery links yourself.

## 4. Share products you recommend

Eligible products can also be promoted by signed-in members. Create a referral link, share it with people who may genuinely find the product useful, and qualifying purchases can generate referral earnings.

Promoting is optional. You can use Cliqero entirely as a customer.

## 5. Track earnings separately

Referral earnings have their own history and availability status. Once earnings become available under the applicable policy, eligible amounts can be withdrawn from your account.

That separation keeps buying products and earning from referrals understandable as two different activities.
