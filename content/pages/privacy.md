Your privacy matters when you use Cliqero. This policy explains the information involved in providing the service and the purposes for which it is used.

## Information associated with your account

When you create and use an account, Cliqero may process information such as your email address, username, profile details, authentication records, account preferences, and information needed to keep your account secure.

## Purchases and access

Cliqero keeps records needed to process purchases, determine the access attached to your account, show your purchase history, and provide support when something goes wrong.

## Payments

When you add funds or use an external payment method, the selected payment provider processes the provider-side payment information required for that transaction. Cliqero keeps the transaction records needed to confirm funding and maintain the corresponding account history.

Do not send passwords, full payment credentials, API keys, or other secrets to support.

## Referrals and earnings

If you use referral features, Cliqero records information needed to create referral links, attribute qualifying activity, calculate applicable earnings, show referral history, and process eligible withdrawals.

## Service and security information

Technical information may be processed when necessary to operate the service, prevent abuse, investigate failures, secure accounts, and maintain reliable application behaviour.

## How information is used

Information is used to operate Cliqero, authenticate users, complete requested transactions, provide purchased access, maintain account histories, support referral features, respond to support requests, protect the platform, and meet applicable operational or legal obligations.

## Third-party services

Some features depend on external services such as payment or authentication providers. Information sent to those providers is also handled under their respective privacy policies and terms.

## Your account information

You can review and update supported profile information from your account. For privacy questions or requests concerning information associated with your account, use the support details on the Contact page.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Cliqero changes. The updated version published on this page applies from its stated update date.
