title: Frequently asked questions
description: Answers to common questions about discovering, buying, accessing, and sharing products on Cliqero.
---

## What is Cliqero?

Cliqero is a catalogue platform where you can discover digital products, purchase access, and return to your purchases from your account. Eligible products can also be shared through referrals.

## Do I need an account to browse?

No. The public catalogue can be explored without signing in. An account is required when you want to purchase, access account-specific features, or promote an eligible product.

## How do I buy a product?

Open a product from the catalogue and choose **Buy**. If you are not signed in, you will be asked to sign in or create an account before continuing. Cliqero then takes you through the checkout required to complete the purchase.

## What happens if I need to add funds?

If your available purchase value is not enough for the item, you can add funds using an available payment provider. Once the funds are confirmed and available, the purchase can continue.

## Where do I find something I already bought?

Your completed purchases are kept in your account. Use the Purchases area to return to the access available for each purchase.

## Can I sell my own products on Cliqero?

No. The catalogue is managed by Cliqero. Regular member accounts are for discovering, buying, accessing, and optionally promoting catalogue products rather than creating commercial listings.

## How do referrals work?

Signed-in members can promote eligible products using their referral links. When a qualifying purchase is correctly attributed to your referral, the applicable commission is recorded as referral earnings.

## Do I have to promote products?

No. Referrals are optional. You can browse and buy products without participating in promotion or earnings features.

## Can I withdraw referral earnings?

Available referral earnings can be withdrawn subject to the withdrawal options and policies shown in your account. Pending earnings must become available before they can be withdrawn.

## Where can I get help?

Visit the Contact page for the current support address and include enough information for the support team to understand the issue. Never send your password, API keys, or payment secrets.
