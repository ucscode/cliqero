Cliqero is a catalogue-led platform for discovering useful digital products, purchasing access, and sharing eligible products with other people.

## A catalogue built for discovery

The catalogue is provided and managed by Cliqero. Members do not need to become sellers or create products before they can use the platform. You can browse what is available, choose what is useful to you, and purchase it directly.

Once a purchase is complete, access belongs to your account so you can return to your purchases without having to rediscover how or where the product was delivered.

## Sharing is optional

Some catalogue items can also be promoted through referral links. If you find something worth recommending, you can share it and may earn from qualifying purchases attributed to your referral.

Buying and promoting are separate choices. You can simply use Cliqero as a customer without participating in referrals.

## Clear by design

Cliqero keeps the important parts of the experience distinct: discovering products, paying for purchases, accessing what you bought, and tracking referral earnings. That separation helps each action remain understandable instead of turning the platform into one complicated flow.

Our aim is simple: make it easy to find something useful, buy it, get access to it, and—when you choose to—share it with others.
